About us

Comprehensive engineering with a global vision

EPM Solutions is an engineering and project management firm founded by Civil Engineer Darío Romero, with a solid track record spanning over 25 years in the comprehensive management of large-scale investment projects across Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Peru, Uruguay, Paraguay, Mexico, and Angola.

We participate in every stage of the project: from planning and design through construction, supervision, and commissioning. Our expertise covers project siting, environmental impact studies, layout and full engineering development, contractor selection and procurement, and overall coordination through to operational handover.
